# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the Zevic community to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before assuming bad intent.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or unwelcome attention.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and chat), and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and ban contributors for behavior they find inappropriate.
